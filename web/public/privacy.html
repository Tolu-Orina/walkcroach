WalkCroach Web Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 July 2026 · Product: WalkCroach Web (app / Chat / Builder)
Publisher site: https://walkcroach.rinegansolutions.com · Chrome extension policy: chrome-privacy.html

WalkCroach Web is an SME builder and creative studio. You chat, build apps, generate slides/flyers/images/short video, and optionally connect third-party accounts. We process what you submit to provide those features — not to scrape your business by default.

1. What we collect

  • Account identifiers (Cognito subject, email when signed in)
  • Chat messages, attachments you upload, project files, and memory you save
  • Creative artefacts you generate (briefs, decks, flyers, stills, ≤30s video) and related job metadata (status, credits charged, quota counters)
  • Usage ledger: credit debits, plan entitlement, Stripe customer id when billing
  • Connector OAuth tokens for accounts you connect (Google, Slack, Stripe Connect, HubSpot) — stored in AWS Secrets Manager, not returned to the browser
  • Operational logs and product metrics (feature counts, latency, error codes)

2. How we use AI models

Inference runs on Amazon Bedrock (Amazon Nova 2 Lite / Pro, Nova Canvas, Nova Reel) and related AWS services (e.g. Polly for video voiceover). Prompts and outputs for creatives may pass through Bedrock Guardrails. We do not sell your prompts to train third-party foundation models.

3. Hard caps & billing

  • Paid plan (~$20/mo) unlocks creatives and connector writes
  • Images: ≤3 / owner / rolling 24 hours (hard cap, even when paid)
  • Video: ≤1 × ≤30s / owner / rolling 72 hours (hard cap)
  • Payments: Stripe Checkout and Customer Portal; webhooks update entitlements

4. Connectors

Connecting Gmail, Calendar, Sheets, Slack, Stripe, or HubSpot is opt-in. Writes use propose → confirm → execute. Disconnecting deletes the stored OAuth token. Workflow run records keep action outcomes for audit, not your credentials.

5. Storage & retention

  • Primary system of record: CockroachDB (never-delete / superseded_by patterns)
  • Artefacts (images, decks, video): object storage with short-lived download URLs
  • Account deletion requests: privacy@walkcroach.com

6. What we do not claim

  • We do not claim unlimited image or video generation
  • We do not claim Canva Autofill or non-Nova creative vendors in-product
  • Free tier does not include Pro orchestration, Canvas, or Reel
  • Failed video jobs free the 72h slot for retry; credits already debited are not auto-refunded

7. Children

WalkCroach Web is intended for business users. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction).

8. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@walkcroach.com
